GRPO

Group Relative Policy Optimization compares several completions for the same prompt. A completion does not need an absolute perfect reward; it needs to beat the other completions in its group.

The Relative Advantage

GRPO normalizes rewards within a sampled group. The group becomes the baseline, so the model gets a preference signal without training a separate value model for each token.

A_i=\frac{r_i-\mathrm{mean}(r_1,\ldots,r_G)}{\mathrm{std}(r_1,\ldots,r_G)+\epsilon}

What This Teaches

GRPO is not just "reward high answers." It is "reward answers that are high relative to other answers to the same prompt." That makes prompt difficulty less of a problem and turns each prompt into its own small comparison game.
