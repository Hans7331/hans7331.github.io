Logistic Regression

Logistic regression turns a linear score into a probability. The important object is not a straight-line prediction; it is the decision boundary created after the sigmoid squashes any score into the range from zero to one.

The Model

The model first computes a log-odds score. The sigmoid transforms that score into a probability, and a threshold turns the probability into a class decision.

p(y=1\mid x)=\sigma(\beta_0+\beta_1x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-(\beta_0+\beta_1x)}}
