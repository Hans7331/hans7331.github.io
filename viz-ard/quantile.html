Quantile Regression

Quantile regression predicts a chosen point in the conditional distribution. The median answers a different question from the 90th percentile, and that matters when the tail risk is the thing you care about.

The Pinball Loss

The loss is asymmetric. If you ask for the 90th percentile, under-predicting is much more expensive than over-predicting. That asymmetry pushes the fitted line toward the requested quantile.

L_\tau(y,\hat y)=\max(\tau(y-\hat y),(\tau-1)(y-\hat y))
