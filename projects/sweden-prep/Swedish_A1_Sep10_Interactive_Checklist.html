2. Måste, får and ska + bare infinitive

These modal verbs are followed by an infinitive without att. The modal is the finite verb; it stays in the same form with every subject.

Three shopping modals
ModalCore meaningExample
måstemust / have toJag måste köpa mat.
fårmay / am allowed to; also getFår jag betala med kort?
skawill / shall / am going toVi ska handla i kväll.

Hon måste köpa mjölk.

She has to buy milk.

Måste du betala nu?

Do you have to pay now?

Får jag prova den här?

May I try this one?

Du får betala i kassan.

You may/can pay at the checkout.

Jag ska köpa två äpplen.

I am going to buy two apples.

Ska ni betala med kort?

Are you going to pay by card?

får inte means “may not / must not”: Du får inte gå in. måste inte normally means “do not have to”: Du måste inte köpa den.

4. Ask about products, prices and payment

Pointing and price patterns
SwedishWhen to use it
Vad kostar den här?What does this one cost? — an en-word
Vad kostar det här?What does this cost? — an ett-word/general item
Vad kostar de här?What do these cost? — plural
Hur mycket kostar mjölken?How much does the milk cost?
Det kostar fyra euro femtio.It costs €4.50.
De kostar tio euro.They cost €10.
Kan jag betala med kort?Can I pay by card?
Jag betalar kontant.I am paying in cash.
Kan jag få kvittot?Can I have the receipt?
In Finnish/Swedish price notation, a comma commonly marks decimals: 4,50 €. Say fyra euro femtio. After a number, euro normally stays unchanged.

5. Listen and role-play in a shop

In a shop

BUrsäkta, var finns mjölken?Excuse me, where is the milk?
ADär borta, bredvid juicen.Over there, next to the juice.
BTack. Vad kostar den här?Thanks. How much is this one?
ATvå euro femtio.Two euros fifty.
BJag tar två. Får jag betala med kort?I’ll take two. May I pay by card?
AJa. Vill du ha kvittot?Yes. Do you want the receipt?
BJa tack.Yes, please.
  1. Listen once and identify the product, place, price, quantity and payment method.
  2. Shadow the customer role, then the shop assistant role.
  3. Replace milk with another product and choose a new price.
  4. Add one measured quantity such as ett kilo äpplen.
  5. Record the changed dialogue and listen for modal + infinitive forms.

6. Quick self-test

7. September 10 milestone — a shop conversation with prices

Perform a shop conversation and ask prices. Play both roles during practice; for the final round, use another person or record both roles. Do not read the full script.

  • Ask where a product is and understand a simple location answer.
  • Ask at least two prices and answer them in Swedish.
  • State a quantity using a number, quantity word or container.
  • Use at least two of måste, får, ska correctly.
  • Choose card or cash and ask for or decline a receipt.

Correct the draft, reduce it to five key words, and then hide it for the final performance.
