6. Build a complete normal day

  • Start with when you wake up and get up.
  • Add breakfast and what you drink.
  • Say when and how you travel to work or school.
  • Describe work/study, lunch and when you finish.
  • Describe the evening and bedtime.
  • Use at least three clock times and three frequency words.

Use subject-first statements today. Tomorrow focuses specifically on fronted-time inversion.
