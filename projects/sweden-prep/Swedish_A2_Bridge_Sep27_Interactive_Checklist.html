2. Listening — main point, then useful details

Do not open the transcript first. Listen once for the situation, again for the reason and new plan, then answer from memory.

Hej! Det är Lina. Jag kan tyvärr inte komma på konserten på fredag eftersom jag arbetar till klockan sju. Kan vi gå på bio på lördag i stället? Ring mig i kväll. Hej då!

Key: Lina; concert on Friday; she works until seven; cinema on Saturday; call her tonight.

5. Focused error repair

Record only errors that affected clarity or repeated patterns. For each one: write the original, the correction, the rule/chunk, and one fresh example.

Aim for 3–5 useful repairs, not a list of every hesitation.

6. September 27 milestone — practical communication lab

  • Reading: purpose + at least four details + a two-sentence summary.
  • Listening: main point + five key details after at least two plays.
  • Writing: one complete practical message that answers every instruction.
  • Speaking: a 1–2 minute problem/solution response, recorded twice.
  • Repair: 3–5 errors converted into correct new examples.
