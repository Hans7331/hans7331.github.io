1. Infinitive or present tense?

The infinitive is the dictionary form. Vocabulary lists often show it with att: att tala (“to speak”). The present tense is the finite verb that tells what happens now or generally: Jag talar svenska.

Learn the two forms as a pair
InfinitivePresentExample now
att talatalarJag talar engelska.
att arbetaarbetarHon arbetar hemma.
att boborVi bor i Finland.
att gågårDe går till jobbet.
att göragörVad gör du?
att seserHan ser en buss.
Do not guess from one ending. Many present forms end in -r, but the stem can change: gå → går, göra → gör, se → ser. Memorise infinitive + present together.

3. Present tense does not change by person

Swedish gives you a major shortcut: a present-tense verb has the same form with every subject. Only the subject changes.

One verb, every subject
Subjectarbetabo
jagjag arbetarjag bor
dudu arbetardu bor
han / honhan arbetar / hon arbetarhan bor / hon bor
vivi arbetarvi bor
nini arbetarni bor
dede arbetarde bor
Do not add English-style endings. It is jag arbetar and hon arbetar, never hon arbetars.

Jag studerar svenska.

I study Swedish.

Du studerar svenska.

You study Swedish.

Hon studerar svenska.

She studies Swedish.

Vi studerar svenska.

We study Swedish.

4. Build a safe basic statement

Core frame: SUBJECT + FINITE VERB + REST. Start with this frame until it feels automatic.
See the three sentence parts
SubjectFinite verbRestComplete sentence
Jagarbetari Helsingfors.Jag arbetar i Helsingfors.
Hondrickerkaffe på morgonen.Hon dricker kaffe på morgonen.
Viläseren bok.Vi läser en bok.
Deköpermat.De köper mat.

The finite verb is the present-tense verb. A statement needs it: Jag bor i Finland. A bare infinitive cannot replace it: Jag att bo i Finland.

  • Choose a subject: jag, du, han, hon, vi, ni, de.
  • Choose one present verb: bor, arbetar, läser, äter.
  • Add useful information: a thing, a place or a time.
  • Say the full sentence once, then write it without copying.

5. Listening and speaking: automate short sentences

Jag heter Lina och jag bor i Åbo.

My name is Lina and I live in Turku.

Jag arbetar hemma och jag studerar svenska.

I work at home and I study Swedish.

Jag dricker kaffe på morgonen.

I drink coffee in the morning.

Vi läser, skriver och talar svenska.

We read, write and speak Swedish.

Han går till jobbet.

He walks/goes to work.

De tittar på en film.

They watch a film.

  1. Listen once without speaking.
  2. Listen again and shadow the voice.
  3. Repeat each sentence alone twice.
  4. Replace the subject or one detail and say the new sentence.
  5. Record six sentences and listen back for the verb forms.

Save only two useful corrections, then repeat the sentence correctly.
