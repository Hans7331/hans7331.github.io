2. Vill, kan and behöver + bare infinitive

vill (want to), kan (can) and behöver (need to) are followed by an infinitive without att in these patterns.

Three high-use patterns
PatternExampleMeaning
vill + infinitiveJag vill äta.I want to eat.
kan + infinitiveJag kan komma.I can come.
behöver + infinitiveJag behöver köpa mat.I need to buy food.
vill ha + nounJag vill ha en kaffe.I would like/want a coffee.
behöver + nounJag behöver hjälp.I need help.
Use the bare infinitive after the modal: Jag kan dricka kaffe, not Jag kan dricker kaffe and not Jag kan att dricka kaffe.

Jag vill äta en sallad.

I want to eat a salad.

Hon vill ha te.

She would like tea.

Kan du hjälpa mig?

Can you help me?

Vi kan betala med kort.

We can pay by card.

De behöver köpa bröd.

They need to buy bread.

Jag behöver inte köpa socker.

I do not need to buy sugar.

3. Polite café requests

Memorise these as complete chunks
SwedishUse
Jag vill ha en kaffe, tack.I would like a coffee, please.
Kan jag få en smörgås, tack?Can I have a sandwich, please?
Jag tar te.I’ll have tea.
Har ni mjölk?Do you have milk?
Utan socker, tack.Without sugar, please.
Något annat?Anything else?
Nej tack, det är bra.No thank you, that is enough/fine.
Vad får det lov att vara?What would you like?
Swedish often uses tack where English uses “please.” En kaffe is a common café order meaning one serving/cup of coffee, even though kaffe is otherwise often uncountable.

Kan jag få kaffe med mjölk, tack?

Can I have coffee with milk, please?

Jag vill ha en smörgås utan ost.

I would like a sandwich without cheese.

Har ni te?

Do you have tea?

Nej tack, inget annat.

No thank you, nothing else.

5. Listen and role-play a complete café dialogue

At a café

AHej, vad får det lov att vara?Hi, what would you like?
BEn kaffe och en smörgås, tack.A coffee and a sandwich, please.
ANågot annat?Anything else?
BNej tack. Hur mycket kostar det?No thanks. How much is it?
AÅtta euro.Eight euros.
BJag betalar med kort.I’ll pay by card.
  1. Listen once for the situation and total price.
  2. Listen again and shadow role B.
  3. Speak role A, leaving time for an imagined customer answer.
  4. Change both ordered items and the price.
  5. Perform both roles once without the English translation.

Note the order, total and no more than three corrections.

7. September 9 milestone — a café interaction without notes

Perform a full café interaction without notes. Practise both customer and server roles, then ask someone to take one role or record both yourself.

  • Greet and ask/order at least one food and one drink.
  • Use a polite request with tack.
  • Respond to Något annat?.
  • Ask and answer the total price.
  • Finish with payment language and thanks.
