3. Var or vart?

var asks about a location: “where is/are?” vart asks about a destination or direction: “where to?”

Var ligger stationen?

Where is the station?

The station has a location.

Var är busshållplatsen?

Where is the bus stop?

The stop has a location.

Vart går du?

Where are you going?

The person has a destination.

Vart åker bussen?

Where is the bus going?

The bus has a destination.
