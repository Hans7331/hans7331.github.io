2. Form the preterite: useful patterns, not guesses

The preterite describes events in a finished past period. Like the present tense, it has the same form for every subject: jag arbetade, hon arbetade, vi arbetade.

These patterns help, but consonant and vowel changes mean you should learn present + preterite as a pair. Do not create an unfamiliar past form by guessing.

Jag arbetade inte i går.

I did not work yesterday.

Negation follows the finite past verb.

I går arbetade jag inte.

Yesterday I did not work.

Time first → finite verb second → subject → inte.

Arbetade du i går?

Did you work yesterday?

Yes/no question begins with the finite verb.

Vad gjorde du i går?

What did you do yesterday?

Question word + finite verb + subject.

5. Connect events into a small narrative

A narrative is more than unrelated sentences. Use först (first), sedan (then), efter det (after that) and till slut (finally) to show order.

Först vaknade jag klockan sju.

First I woke at seven.

Sedan åt jag frukost.

Then I ate breakfast.

Efter det åkte jag buss till jobbet.

After that I took the bus to work.

Till slut gick jag hem och lagade mat.

Finally I went home and cooked.
