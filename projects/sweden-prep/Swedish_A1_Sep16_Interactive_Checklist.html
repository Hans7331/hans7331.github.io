2. Say how you feel and describe simple symptoms

SwedishEnglish

Hur mår du?How are you?
Jag mår bra.I am well.
Jag mår dåligt.I feel unwell.
Jag är sjuk.I am ill.
Jag har ont i huvudet.I have a headache.
Jag har ont i magen.My stomach hurts.
Jag har ont i ryggen.My back hurts.
Use the fixed frame Jag har ont i + definite body part: huvudet, magen, ryggen, foten. Do not translate English “I am pain” word for word.

4. Build a symptom + appointment interaction

  • Greet the receptionist and say you want an appointment.
  • Say simply how you feel or where you have pain.
  • Listen to or invent one proposed day and time.
  • Accept it, or reject it politely and ask for morning/afternoon.
  • Confirm and thank the receptionist.

With the neutral weather subject det, adjective forms take -t: varmt, kallt, soligt, regnigt, molnigt.

7. Listening-and-response rehearsal

Use the audio buttons above or ask a partner/device to read a dialogue. Listen once for the situation, again for the symptom or weather, and a third time for the day, time or temperature.

  1. First pass: identify “appointment” or “weather”.
  2. Second pass: note the key symptom or weather condition.
  3. Third pass: note the exact day/time or temperature.
  4. Answer aloud in one or two simple Swedish sentences.
