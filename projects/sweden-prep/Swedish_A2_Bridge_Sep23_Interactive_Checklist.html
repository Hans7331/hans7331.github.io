1. Choose the future pattern that matches your meaning

Bridge rule: start this page only if your September 20 result was “A1 workbook skills are comfortable.” If you selected “I need 3–7 focused review days” or “I need more foundation work,” repair the named A1 skills first.
Four ways to talk about the future
PatternUseModel
ska + infinitivea plan, intention or arrangementJag ska studera i kväll. — I am going to study tonight.
tänker + infinitivewhat someone intends to doJag tänker resa i sommar. — I intend to travel this summer.
kommer att + infinitivea prediction or future factDet kommer att regna. — It is going to rain.
present tense + future timea fixed timetable or clear arrangementTåget går klockan åtta i morgon. — The train leaves at eight tomorrow.
After ska and tänker, use the infinitive without att. In kommer att resa, att belongs to the future construction.

3. Hear and compare the four meanings

Jag ska köpa en biljett i kväll.

I am going to buy a ticket tonight.

A personal plan.

Jag tänker åka till Åbo i helgen.

I intend to go to Turku this weekend.

An intention.

Resan kommer att bli rolig.

The trip is going to be fun.

A prediction.

Bussen avgår klockan nio på lördag.

The bus departs at nine on Saturday.

A fixed timetable.

Vi ska inte stanna länge.

We are not going to stay long.

inte follows ska.

När kommer du att boka hotellet?

When will you book the hotel?

Question word + finite verb + subject.

4. Plan-message workshop

A useful invitation answers four things: what, when, where, and what response you want.

Hej Leo! Jag tänker gå på konsert på lördag. Konserten börjar klockan sju i Kulturhuset. Ska du följa med? Vi kan träffas utanför klockan halv sju. Svara gärna i kväll! Hälsningar, Mia

The message states the plan, event time/place, invitation and requested response.

Include what, when, where, a question/request, and at least two different future patterns.
