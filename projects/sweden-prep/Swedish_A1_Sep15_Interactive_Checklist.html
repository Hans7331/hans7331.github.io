3. Imperatives: direct, useful instruction forms

The imperative tells someone to do something. Common forms vary, so learn these high-frequency commands as complete chunks rather than guessing.

ImperativeEnglishUseful expansion
Kom!Come!Kom hit!
Vänta!Wait!Vänta lite!
Lyssna!Listen!Lyssna och upprepa!
Titta!Look!Titta på tavlan!
Läs!Read!Läs texten!
Skriv!Write!Skriv ditt namn!
Ta!Take!Ta en penna!
Ring!Call!Ring chefen!

7. Prepare 8–10 sentences about your work or studies

  • Say whether you work, study, both, or neither.
  • Name the workplace, school, course or subject.
  • Say where it is and when you begin and finish.
  • Mention a teacher, boss, colleague or classmate if relevant.
  • Describe two normal actions with work/study verbs.
  • Say what you like, need, can or want to do.
