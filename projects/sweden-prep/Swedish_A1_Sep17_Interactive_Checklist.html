2. Say what you like — and what you do not like

Jag gillar musik.

I like music.

gillar + noun

Jag gillar att simma.

I like swimming.

gillar att + infinitive

Jag tycker om att laga mat.

I like cooking.

tycker om att + infinitive

Jag gillar inte fotboll.

I do not like football.

inte follows the finite verb

Jag tycker inte om att springa.

I do not like running.

Keep tycker … om together in meaning; inte follows tycker

På helgen spelar jag gitarr.

At the weekend I play guitar.

Fronted time phrase triggers inversion

Useful contrast: Jag gillar kaffe is a preference. Jag vill ha kaffe means you want coffee now.

Use at least one noun pattern, one att + infinitive pattern and one fronted time phrase.

5. Retrieval quiz: make the language work

6. Build and rehearse the two-minute talk

  1. Who: name, origin, home, language, work/studies.
  2. What: two or three hobbies and when/how often you do them.
  3. Why: one simple reason, for example Det är roligt.
  4. Contrast: one thing you do not like.
  5. Plan: what you want to do next weekend.

Useful starters: På fritiden…, Ofta…, Ibland…, Men jag gillar inte…, Nästa helg vill jag…
